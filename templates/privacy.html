Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated_date }}

This policy explains how Duding ("we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information through duding.ai (the "Site") and our lead-intake and outreach services (the "Service"). It does not apply to CHKD (getchkd.app), which is a separate product with its own privacy policy. The Instagram Graph API integration described in Section 2a is an exception to the above and applies regardless of which client's account is connected, since the integration itself is operated on duding.ai's infrastructure.

1. Who we are

Duding builds and operates automated lead-intake systems for local service businesses (plumbers, HVAC, electricians, roofers, and similar trades). Contact: {{ contact_email }}.

2. Information we collect

From businesses who submit a lead or contact form: name, email, business name, budget range, website link, and any other details you provide.

From businesses we contact directly: we identify prospective local service businesses using publicly available information (business name, publicly listed contact email, and public website content) found through web search and public business websites. If you did not sign up directly and received an email from us, this is why — see Section 5 on how to stop receiving these emails.

From clients who purchase a build or retainer: business details, payment information (processed by Stripe — we do not store your card number), ad account and social media login details you choose to share for retainer/marketing services, and communications with us.

Automatically: standard server logs (IP address, request timestamps, pages requested) and session cookies used to keep you signed in to the admin dashboard.

2a. Instagram Graph API integration

We connect Instagram Business accounts — including our own and, where applicable, accounts operated on behalf of clients such as Get CHKD — via Meta's Instagram Graph API to read content performance data (reach, engagement, watch time, and audience insights) for posts that account has published. This data is used solely to inform content strategy for that account. It is never shared with third parties, never used for advertising or ad targeting, and no data belonging to any other Instagram account or user is accessed. Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the account in Meta's Business Integrations settings or by contacting us at the address in Section 10.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to lead submissions and provide quotes/builds.
  • To send outreach emails to prospective business clients about our services.
  • To deliver purchased services (website builds, retainer marketing services) and process payments.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site and Service.

4. Who we share information with

We share information with the service providers that power the Service, and only as needed for them to provide that service to us:

  • Resend — email delivery.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe receives your payment details directly; we do not see or store full card numbers.
  • Railway — application hosting and database infrastructure.
  • Anthropic — used internally to help generate marketing content and business research for retainer clients; not used to process lead or payment data.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Outreach emails and opt-out

If you received a cold email from us, it's because we identified your business as a potential fit for our services using publicly available contact information. Every outreach email includes instructions to opt out — replying "unsubscribe" removes you from future contact. We do not re-contact a business domain once it has opted out.

6. Data retention

We retain lead, prospect, and client records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Service or as required by law (e.g. financial records related to payments). You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing {{ contact_email }}.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact {{ contact_email }}.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on administrative systems. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: {{ contact_email }}.
